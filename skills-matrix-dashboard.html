How I developed people

  1. SOPs firstTraining started from the standard operating procedure on the critical processes.
  2. Buddy upEach person was paired with an operative who had years of experience on the process.
  3. Quality auditQuality audits made sure the job was done right.
  4. Widen the skillsOnce someone reached the level the company required, they trained on other processes where the single point of failure risk was highest.

Then the cycle started again, until there were no gaps left.

Evidence behind the numbers

  • Built from scratchBuilt the skills matrix covering 260 critical processes and led a team of 29.
  • Cross-training that landedThe 3x3 programme took three job roles from about 70% to 100% competency in five months.
  • Coverage you can verifySkills coverage rose from 36% to 86.6% over two years, with 348 of 401 processes at Operating status across my direct team of 11.
  • Engagement in safety108 near-miss reports raised through SafetyQube, an EHS management system, closed at 99.7%, with a 22% uplift in site-wide near-miss reporting.
