The rules I hold

AI output can look right and be wrong. These are the habits that stop that reaching the shop floor.

  • A person is responsible for the result. Claude drafts. I read it, test it and own it.
  • Gaps are shown, not hidden. A tool says "Gaps found. Do not rely on this" when its checks fail. It never says something is safe or compliant.
  • Example data is labelled. Anything that is not a real figure says so on the page.
  • Safety tools get a higher bar. Tools that deal with people's lives are checked more closely than the rest.
  • Every figure names its source. My tracker, my CV or my scrap record, and what has not been checked against company records.
