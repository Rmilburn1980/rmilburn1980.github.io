A five level lens for how well a workplace tracks, interprets and acts on changing EHS law, from scattered site by site checking to forward looking horizon scanning. Score a site you have worked in, or use it to ask sharper questions of an employer.

How to use this
  • Open a level to see what it looks like, what holds a workplace there and what moves it on.

  • Your answers save in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The questions and score bands are my own adaptation, so the result is a conversation tool, not an audit or a benchmark.
