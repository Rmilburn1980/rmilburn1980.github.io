Teams

What needs attention first

Skills with the same name in different teams are grouped. The coverage is the number of people signed off in date, against the number each team said it needs.

What it does

It reads the matrices that teams have built and applies the same rules to each one. It does not take anyone's word for a level: a level 3 or 4 counts only when a named assessor other than the operator has signed it off and the date is inside the valid period set in that team's matrix.

The measures

  • Cover against target: for each skill, the signed-off people counted up to the number the team said it needs, added up across the team, as a share of the total needed.
  • Critical skills covered: critical skills that have at least as many signed-off people as the team needs.
  • Claimed but not counted: people shown as competent (level 3 or 4) whose sign-off is missing, self-signed, or out of date. A high number means the matrix looks better than the evidence.
  • Expiring in 90 days: signed-off people whose sign-off runs out within three months. Plan re-checks before cover drops.
  • Key person risks: people who are the only signed-off cover for three or more skills.
  • File age: a team file saved more than 90 days ago is flagged, because the figures may be out of date.

Limits

No gaps found does not mean the company is competent. The dashboard checks the record, not the people. Teams may define skills and targets differently, so compare with care, and have a competent person judge the sign-offs. Check your own legal duties on training and competence.
