DMAIC Project Board

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt project: First Pass Yield at Stage 8, caravan assembly line
Richard Milburn, Erwin Hymer Group UK (Elddis), Cell Leader
Objective: raise Stage 8 First Pass Yield from a low-50s baseline to a sustained 96%+

1Define

Problem statement

Stage 8 First Pass Yield was inconsistent and frequently below target, driving rework, delayed sign-off, and Damaged-in-Production scrap cost above budget.

Project charter

  • Project: Improve FPY at Stage 8
  • Method: DMAIC
  • Scope: Caravan assembly, Stage 8 Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) checkpoint
  • Team: Production, Quality, Engineering (Design Change Requests, DCR)

SIPOC (summary)

Suppliers: Goods-in, upstream stages
Inputs: Torque wrench cal., operator competency, jig cal., DCR status
Process: Stage 8 assembly & PDI check
Outputs: Pass/fail status, fault category
Customers: PDI, downstream stages, end customer

Voice of the customer / critical to quality

  • First-time pass rate
  • Consistent, auditable measurement
  • Reduced DiP scrap cost

The low 50s is the starting baseline and 96%+ is the sustained peak. The 68.8% and 82.4% figures are the averages either side of the programme.

Data collection plan

2 Y variables, 4 X variables tracked:

  • Y: Pass/fail status (Avante system)
  • Y: Fault category
  • X: Torque wrench calibration
  • X: Operator competency stage
  • X: Jig/fixture calibration
  • X: DCR status

Measurement system

All faults logged on Avante, including on-the-spot corrections. Vans cannot receive final sign-off until every fault clears, which gives an auditable, gap-free measure of first-time pass performance.

FPY trend (illustrative)

Shape reflects the real baseline to sustained 96%+ improvement. A formal weekly tracking gap existed between wk3 and wk16 before the programme was fully established.

Root cause (5 Why style)

Fault: torque-related rework at Stage 8
Why? No continuous torque reading recorded
Why? Wrench calibration tracked as pass/fail only
Why? No formal continuous calibration log existed

Regression check

Weekly FPY vs time: quadratic fit R² = 0.275. Time alone is a weak predictor, which suggests the process inputs above, not the passage of time, are the drivers.

4Improve

Interventions

  • Shop-floor teams given direct ownership of quality at Stage 8
  • Torque wrench & jig calibration upgraded from attribute (in/out) to continuous logging
  • DCR escalation used to route recurring faults to Engineering for design-level fix
  • Cross-functional 3x3 training raised operator capability from about 70% to 100% across 3 roles in 5 months

Result

low 50s to 96%+

Sustained, with individual weeks reaching 100%.

Lessons learned

  • Clear direction and genuine team ownership drove results, not top-down enforcement alone
  • Continuous data beats attribute data even when it costs more upfront to set up
  • Data-driven root cause outperforms inspection/rework as the fix
