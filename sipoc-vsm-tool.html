2 SIPOC

A one-page picture of the process before you map any detail. Agree the start and end points with the people involved.

How do I fill this in?
  • Give the process a name, and say what starts it and where it ends.
  • Fill in Process first: 4 to 7 main steps, in order.
  • Then Outputs and Customers: what the process produces and who gets it.
  • Finally Inputs and Suppliers: what the process needs and who provides it.
  • Tip: press Enter to add the next item, and Backspace on an empty item to remove it.

3 Process steps and times

Walk the process and record what you see, step by step. Rough numbers are fine to start with, and you can refine them later.

What do these terms mean?
Cycle time
How often one finished item leaves a step. If 6 items leave every hour, the cycle time is 10 minutes.
Wait before step
How long an item sits waiting before the step starts.
Queue before step
How many items are waiting there.
Takt time
The pace the customer needs. Available time divided by demand. A step with a longer cycle time than takt cannot keep up.
Changeover or setup
Time lost switching between products or jobs.
Uptime or availability
The share of time the step is ready to work, as a percentage.
First pass yield
The percentage of items done right first time, with no rework.
Value-added time
The part of the cycle time the customer would pay for. Leave it blank to count the whole cycle time.
