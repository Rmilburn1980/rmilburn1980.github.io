Observer assessment

Observe the task for 20 to 30 cycles before answering. Score the worst case for each body area. If in doubt, choose the higher exposure category.

Worker assessment

The worker answers from their own experience of the task. If the worker is in doubt, choose the higher category.

Method: Quick Exposure Check, Li, G. and Buckle, P. (1999), Evaluating Change in Exposure to Risk for Musculoskeletal Disorders, a Practical Tool, HSE Books CRR251. Scoring grids and body area levels follow the QEC reference guide.

Overall exposure is the sum of the four body area scores as a percentage of the maximum possible: 162 for a static back task, 176 for a manual handling back task. Interactions scoring 8 or more are flagged, and 10 or 12 should be addressed urgently.

Scores support comparison before and after an intervention and help set priorities. They do not predict injury for an individual.
